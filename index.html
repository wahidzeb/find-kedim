About Find Kedim!

Find Kedim! is a calm hidden cat game with Kedim, the orange cat, and all of Kedim's friends. Each picture is a busy hand drawn place: a kampung with stilt houses, a beach, a KL street, a wet market and a mosque courtyard. Pinch to zoom in, drag to look around and tap every cat you spot.

The first pictures have about 10 easy cats out in the open. Later pictures hide 30 or more, peeking out from windows, under cars and behind stalls, some in colours that blend in. Stuck? Tap the glowing magnifier and it shows you where to look. Every finished picture gives you a sticker for your album, and you can replay any picture against the clock.

3 quick tips

  1. Zoom in close and sweep slowly from one side to the other.
  2. Look for little ears and tails peeking out behind things.
  3. Later cats can be the same colour as walls or sand, so look for the eyes.
